# DetailScope Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-11

## Summary
DetailScope collects no data. Nothing leaves your device.

## What it accesses
- **Page text** on sites you visit: read only to check your rules. Checked locally; never stored or sent.
- **Your rules and settings**: saved in the browser's local extension storage on your device.

## What it does not do
- No data collection, analytics or tracking.
- No servers, no network requests, no remote code.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

## Removing data
Uninstall the extension to delete your rules and settings.

## Changes
Updates to this policy are posted here, with a new effective date.
