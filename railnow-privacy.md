RailNOW Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how RailNOW ("the app", package com.yapps.railnow) handles your data.

1. Data we collect
The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. The app has no internet access.

2. Location
With your permission, the app reads your approximate (coarse) location on your device. It uses it only to decide which of your two saved stations is closer, so it can show trains in the right direction. Your location is not stored and never leaves your device. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time; the app still works, and you can switch direction manually.

3. Data stored on your device
The app stores your two selected stations and your last travel direction on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the system may include these settings in your device backup with Google. The developer has no access to this data.

4. Third parties
The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data. No data is shared with any third party.

5. Timetable data
Train times are public data from Israel's National Public Transport Authority, bundled inside the app. They are planned times and may differ from actual service.

6. Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

7. Deleting your data
Uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings to remove everything it stored.

8. Changes
We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
